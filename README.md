# Lottery Sambad — All State Lottery Results in One Place

Lottery Sambad is an independent, unofficial reference for state lottery results in India. We publish winning numbers from the official announcements as fast as they are declared, with full prize-wise lists and a free ticket checker.

## States covered

- Nagaland Dear Lottery — 3 draws daily: 1 PM, 6 PM, 8 PM. 1st prize ₹1 Crore. Ticket ₹7.
- Kerala State Lotteries — daily 3 PM. 1st prize up to ₹1 Crore. Ticket ₹40–50.
- Sikkim — Labhlaxmi 4 PM (4-digit, 1st prize ₹10,000) + Dear 6 PM (₹1 Crore).

- Maharashtra — weekly draws 4:15–5:00 PM.

## How to check a ticket

1. Match your draw name, date and slot with the published result.
2. Check every prize tier, not just the jackpot — lower tiers (last 4 digits) produce most wins.
3. Verify a big win against the official gazette before claiming. Prizes above ₹10,000 attract 30% TDS.
