# Lottery Prize Tiers Explained: What 'Last 4 Digits Match' Really Means

Every day across India, people tear up lottery tickets that actually won money. Not the jackpot — that one gets checked obsessively. The losses happen in the lower tiers, where a ticket can win ₹500, ₹250 or ₹120 simply because its last four digits match a published list.

## Nagaland Dear Lottery

₹7 ticket, 3 draws daily at 1 PM, 6 PM, 8 PM. 132 winning numbers per draw across six tiers: 1st ₹1 Crore (series + all 5 digits, exact); Consolation ₹1,000 (same 5 digits, different series); 2nd ₹10,000 (10 numbers); 3rd ₹500, 4th ₹250, 5th ₹120 (last 4 digits: 10 + 10 + 100 numbers). Last-4-digit tiers give roughly a 1.2% chance per ticket — about 1 in 83 — the only tier where wins happen regularly.

## Kerala State Lottery

Daily 3 PM, ₹40–50 ticket. Two-letter series + six-digit number, nine tiers: 1st ₹1 Crore; Consolation ₹5,000; 2nd ₹25–30 Lakh; 3rd ₹5 Lakh; 4th ₹5,000; 5th ₹2,000; 6th ₹1,000; 7th ₹500; 8th ₹200; 9th ₹100 — the last six tiers all match on last 4 digits. The 9th prize alone typically publishes 144–150 winning endings per draw.

## Sikkim Labhlaxmi

Daily 4 PM. The simplest format: a plain 4-digit number, six tiers, no series — 1st ₹10,000; 2nd ₹2,000; 3rd ₹500; 4th ₹300; 5th ₹200; 6th ₹100 (360 numbers). Sikkim also conducts the 6 PM Dear draw (₹1 Crore), the same draw as Nagaland's evening slot.

## Three checking mistakes that cost real money

1. Checking only the jackpot — the least likely way to win.
2. Forgetting the series rule cuts both ways — a different series on the jackpot number is the consolation prize, not 'no win'.
3. Checking the wrong draw — draw name, date and slot must all match your ticket.

## Tax

Any prize above ₹10,000 attracts a flat 30% TDS deducted before payment (₹1 Crore becomes ₹70 Lakh in hand). Prizes up to ₹10,000 can be claimed from the retailer; larger prizes go through the state lottery department within 30 days of the draw.
